3D furniture models (CC0 / public domain):

- Kenney Furniture Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) — loungeChair, chairDesk,
  table, sideTable, desk, lampRoundFloor, lampSquareTable, pottedPlant, rugRectangle,
  bedSingle, bookcase*, cabinetTelevision, bathroomMirror, doorwayOpen, wallWindow.
- Local multi-mesh GLBs (sofa, stool, beanbag, pendant, backdrop, projector_screen,
  string_lights) generated for catalog types not in the Kenney subset.

Web loads GLB via drei useGLTF, scaled to catalog dimensions (meters).
iOS Live AR / map twin: per-SKU RealityKit composites (`FurnitureMeshBuilder`) at the
same dimensions; optional USDZ under `apps/ios/SharedSpatialAI/Models/` (same stems).
